# Inclusion and exclusion boundary

**Included:** independently written, small-scale terrain code; synthetic raster and AOI generated from a disclosed formula; generic architecture and public roadmap; generated plot; analytical tests; and example commands.

**Excluded:** all files from the private engine checkout, original or retained client/pilot data, municipality-specific workflows, providers and credentials, production startup/deployment configuration, private research/model logic and weights, commercial notes, confidential roadmaps, LAS/LAZ, and large rasters.

The separate local cuttings example is also excluded because it concerns a different research domain. The showcase makes no claim that synthetic tests validate field accuracy, hydraulic adequacy, subsurface conditions, or professional acceptance.
